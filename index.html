Gold Award, Renovation.

Our most recent honor: a national Gold Award for a project that cost over $1M and took more than a year to build. It meant retaining a steep hillside, then carving in a 40-foot water slide, cascading waterfalls, a shallow children's pool, a Baja ledge, and an infinity edge on both pool and spa.

The kind of brief most builders walk away from. The kind we live for.

The Process

From raw hillside to finished water.

A pool is the easy part. The reason an estate water environment costs what it does is everything underneath it: caissons drilled into unstable hillside, hand-sculpted gunite rockwork, structural engineering that has to outlast the house. Here is one build, start to finish.
