What We Do

Three disciplines.
One uncompromising studio.

01 — Design

Design that solves the site first.

Every property has one ideal pool, and a hundred wrong ones. We start with the land: the grade, the view, the light, the way you'll actually live outside. Then we model it in 3D so you see the finished water before we ever break ground.

  • Infinity & vanishing edges
  • Vanishing acrylic walls & windows
  • Glass tile, fire & water features
  • Full outdoor-living & kitchen integration
02 — Build

Engineered for the lots nobody else will touch.

Hillsides, cliff edges, cantilevers over a canyon. Four decades of gunite construction means we build structures that hold their line for generations. One crew, one standard, no subcontracted shortcuts.

  • Steel-reinforced gunite construction
  • Hillside & caisson engineering
  • Acrylic-wall & infinity structural work
  • Permitting & geotechnical coordination
03 — Rockwork & Remodel

Rock that fools geologists. Remodels that erase decades.

Our hand-carved artificial rock, grottos, and waterfalls are the work we're known for, the reason TV producers and estate owners call. We also bring tired pools back to life, transforming dated builds into modern showpieces.

  • Hand-carved artificial rock & grottos
  • Multi-tier waterfalls & swim-up caves
  • Full pool remodels & resurfacing
  • Rock re-staining & restoration
